The Idea

Why India needs unified land intelligence

India's land records are spread across 36 different systems. A citizen, bank or court has no single place to search, verify and understand a land parcel.

⚠

The Challenge

  • 36 disparate state & UT land registries
  • No unified search across states
  • Presumptive titles — not guaranteed ownership
  • Cadastral maps rarely digitally verified
◈

NLIP's Approach

  • Unified search by ULPIN, Survey No., Khasra, Patta
  • GIS boundary verification against cadastral maps
  • Cross-referenced ownership & encumbrance data
  • Certified Parcel Dossier — comprehensive governance record
How it Works

From thirty-six systems to one answer

Land is a state subject. Every state keeps its own records, in its own format. NLIP unifies them.
