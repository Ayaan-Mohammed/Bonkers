How one record reaches every office

Land is a state subject — every state keeps its own records, in its own format. This is the chain that turns thirty-six different systems into one answer.

  1. 1
    State & department dataRevenue · registration · tax · cadastral · legal — each in its own schema
  2. 2
    Interoperability layerOpen APIs, shared data standards, validation, sync
  3. 3
    Unified national land platformOne record per parcel, keyed to its ULPIN
  4. 4
    Land intelligence & GIS verificationBoundary, ownership and consistency checks
  5. 5
    Digital land passport & assessment tallyThe record, and how much of it checks out
  6. 6
    Citizens & government officialsThe same record, shown to whoever is entitled to see it

This platform shows a presumptive record of rights, not a legal certificate of ownership. Court and dispute data shown here is illustrative. Records reflect the last available sync from each source system.
